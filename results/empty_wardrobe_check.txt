Here are two easy, everyday looks using common basics:

**Look 1: Streetwear Edge**
Pair the baby tee with **light-wash, baggy denim cargo pants** for a classic Y2K silhouette. Add **white canvas low-top sneakers** and a **pastel pink nylon shoulder bag** to tie the butterfly print together. 

**Look 2: Sweet & Casual**
Tuck the tee into a **black pleated tennis skirt** for a cute contrast. Layer with a **white zip-up hoodie** (worn open) and step into **black chunky platform sandals** or classic sneakers. Add silver hoop earrings to finish the fit.
